    Wheels of Trust is a registered trademark of Hero Motocorp that offers services related to:

    I] Online Resale Price Estimation

    Customers can check the estimated resale price of their 2W on the wheels of trust website. Wheels of Trust bears no liability on this indicative price matching with the exact market offer as the same depends on professional health assessment and dynamic market demand.     II] Vehicle Certification

    Customers can get their two-wheeler inspected through a mobile-based application and get a detailed certification report. In this report, customers get an exhaustive checklist based inspection findings and the calibrated ratings for objective analysis of health condition.

    The certification report generated is based on the observations entered by the inspection executive and Wheels of Trust bears no liability over any loss incurred through the observations mentioned in the report or indicative price prediction.

    III] Price Discovery

    Based on the health certification, customers can get their two-wheeler flashed for tendering among a set of verified buyers. This process helps the buyers and sellers to connect digitally and deliver a competitive price-discovery process.

    Wheels of Trust is just a platform to facilitate this process and has no liability over the prices being quoted and physical transaction happening between the buyer and the seller.
